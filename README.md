# Bike_Sales_Project_Abigailoluwaloshe.xlsx.
In this capstone project, you will apply everything you have learned to analyze a bike sales dataset. The goal is to clean, transform, and visualize the data to generate insights. 

Dataset Overview: As part of our strategic initiatives to optimize sales performance, enhance menu offerings, and improve overall customer experience, this food outlet requires a comprehensive analysis of its Pizza Sales Data for the year 2015. The objective is to leverage data-driven insights to identify key trends, optimize operational efficiency, and refine our product offerings to meet customer preferences effectively.
